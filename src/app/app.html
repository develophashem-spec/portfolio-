A little about me

Thoughtful work, built with care.

I’m a Computer Science graduate focused on front-end development, data, and business systems. I enjoy taking a practical problem and shaping it into a clear, considered digital experience.

My experience spans web interfaces, CRM and ERP data, reporting, and email marketing. That mix helps me think about both the experience people use and the systems behind it.

Applying for Lifecycle Marketing Strategist

I’m Ali Hachem, based in Beirut, and I’m applying for the Lifecycle Marketing Strategist role. My background is rooted in customer data, CRM, and performance analysis.

At Rocket Delivery, I managed the company ERP and CRM database and built Power BI reports from it. At Salem Group, I pulled data from multiple sources to create full client proposals covering scope, tools, and budget. I currently work as a Business Development Representative at Vanguard Services, where I handle customer and prospect calls and lead generation through tools such as LinkedIn Sales Navigator and SignalHire, so I hear firsthand how different messages land with people.

I’ve also worked in Mailchimp and Klaviyo, and I use SQL and Python to pull segments and review the numbers myself. What drew me to this role is that it is about diagnosing what is working and why, not just sending campaigns. I’m coming from the data and CRM side, and I’m ready to take on the strategy work quickly while continuing to learn.
